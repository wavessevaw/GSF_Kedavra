## What's New in 1.6.0

- **Kedavra Focus:** Adaptive edge sharpening with halo protection.
- **Simplified Controls:** One ON/OFF switch, fixed 100% strength.
- **Frame Generation:** Focus works on real and generated frames.
- **Upscaler Compatibility:** Independent of FSR, XeSS, DLSS and OptiScaler.
- **GPU Efficiency:** Single-pass processing without extra full-resolution buffers.

*Focus is SDR-only. HDR is bypassed.*
